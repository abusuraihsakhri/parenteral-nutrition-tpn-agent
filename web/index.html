Macronutrient energy calculator

Reconcile an already selected energy target with amino acids, 20% intravenous lipid emulsion, and the remaining dextrose calories.

Educational arithmetic only — not a prescription. No individualized energy requirements, electrolyte composition, fluid adequacy, osmolarity, metabolic risk, or refeeding safety is assessed. A clinician must independently verify the input targets and product label before any clinical use.
